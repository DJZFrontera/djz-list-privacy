DJZ List Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

DJZ List is a local listing-preparation application used by DJZ Finds. This policy describes the application's current data handling, including optional eBay seller authorization.

Information Used

Draft listings, SKUs, seller notes, photographs, corrections, and revision history are stored on the operator's Windows computer. With seller consent, DJZ List uses eBay Production authorization to retrieve existing seller business policies and account information permitted by eBay. It does not currently publish listings or create, modify, or delete eBay policies.

Authorization and Storage

The seller signs in directly through eBay. DJZ List does not collect the seller's eBay password. Access and refresh tokens are stored locally using Windows DPAPI encryption. Authorization codes and tokens are not intended to appear in public exports or application logs. Retrieved policy details may be cached locally.

Service Providers

eBay processes authorization and API requests. When the operator requests Gemini photograph analysis, selected product photographs, internal information-board photographs, and relevant listing inputs are submitted to the configured Google Gemini service. eBay OAuth tokens are not included in those analysis requests.

Cloudflare hosts a separate eBay account-deletion notification receiver. This privacy-policy page is hosted through GitHub Pages.

Retention and Deletion

Local listing data and history remain until removed by the operator. Backups may retain previous copies. Disconnecting the eBay seller account removes locally stored authorization information through the application's disconnect process; revocation of authorization with eBay may require action in eBay account settings.

The Cloudflare notification receiver records eBay account-deletion notifications. Received events may be queued pending processing. Automatic deletion across all local data and backups has not yet been verified. Receipt of a notification must not be interpreted as confirmation that deletion has been completed.

Contact and Questions

DJZ Finds operates DJZ List. For questions about this application or requests concerning stored information, contact DJZ Finds through eBay's seller-contact features.

Changes

This policy will be updated as DJZ List's features and data-handling practices change.
